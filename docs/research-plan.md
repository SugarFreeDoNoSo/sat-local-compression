# Research plan

## Foundations

The initial draft gives definitions, expository proofs, and finite examples.
Next, obtain human review of the evaluator, advice conversion, and encoding
conventions. A convenient notation is not itself a lower-bound technique.

## Restricted models

Select a precise diagram, branching-program, or circuit model. State allowed
orders, encodings, and sharing mechanisms. Identify an explicit hard family.
The present unit-clause example is not hard independently of input order.

## Transfer questions

Record source and target problems, reduction direction, input-size change,
promises, model overhead, and quantifiers. Restricted bounds do not
automatically magnify.

## Article contribution

Decide whether the contribution is a new theorem, model comparison,
documented obstruction, or reproducible observation before selecting a venue.
